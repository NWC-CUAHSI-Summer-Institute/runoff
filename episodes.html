Flash flood episodes i

Browse every weather-caused flash flood episode in the lower 48, pick episodes by rainfall and impact criteria, and download their events, Local Storm Reports, and MRMS rainfall tables. No stream gage required.

The episode catalog has not been built in this copy of the site.
Build it from the repository root (Python 3.10+, pandas):
python engine/lsr/fetch_lsrs.py --wy 2021 2025
python engine/episodes/build_catalog.py --stormevents data/stormevents
then reload this page. MRMS rainfall columns are added later, at your pace, by python engine/episodes/mrms_stats.py. See engine/episodes/README.md.

Rainfall criteria (MRMS) i
Weather-caused flash floods only; dam and levee failure reports are excluded. Event locations are NWS report coordinates, not storm centers; the episode footprint and MRMS rainfall describe the storm itself. Source: NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, NWS Local Storm Reports via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NOAA MRMS on AWS.
