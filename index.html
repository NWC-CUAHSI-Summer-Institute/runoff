Welcome to RUNOFF

Radar and USGS Networked Observations of Flash Floods

RUNOFF is a community benchmark dataset for flash flood modeling in the United States. It pairs thousands of reported flash flood events with radar precipitation, USGS streamflow observations, and documented impacts, all linked to the NOAA NextGen HydroFabric.

RUNOFF

A flash flood benchmark dataset built at the CUAHSI and NOAA National Water Center Summer Institute 2026 by Team "Don't Runoff!". Precipitation from the NOAA Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor system, streamflow from the U.S. Geological Survey, events from the NOAA Storm Events Database, on the NextGen HydroFabric.
