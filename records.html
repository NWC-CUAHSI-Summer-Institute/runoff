RUNOFF

Flash flood impact records from the NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database, mapped by county and by HUC8 watershed as part of the RUNOFF flash flood benchmark dataset. Built at the CUAHSI and NOAA National Water Center Summer Institute 2026 by Team "Don't Runoff!".
